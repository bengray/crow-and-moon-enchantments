My Practice

Crow and Moon Enchantments is just me — Nadine. I spent years as a firefighter and EMT, running toward emergencies while everyone else ran the other way. These days I work with fire on friendlier terms: pouring small-batch candles, blending perfumes and oils, and making the kind of goods that help a home feel a little more enchanted.


You'll find me at popup craft fairs all around the Chicago area. Come say hi — everything on the table was made by hand, and I'm happy to tell you what's in it.
